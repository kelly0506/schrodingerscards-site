Built for Pokémon

Grading yourself, honestly

How to judge condition

Take the card out of the sleeve, hold it under a bright light, and tilt it. Most damage only shows at an angle. Check the front, the back, and all four corners.

Near Mint / Mint

Looks like it just came out of the pack. Sharp corners, clean edges with no white showing, no surface scratches or print lines, and centering that looks even. A tiny factory flaw is still Near Mint.

Lightly Played

Minor wear you have to look for. Slight edge whitening, a small corner softness, or very light surface scuffing. Still looks good in a sleeve.

Moderately Played

Obvious wear at a glance. Noticeable edge whitening, rounded corners, light scratching, or minor creasing that doesn't break the card's surface.

Heavily Played / Damaged

Heavy whitening, real creases, bends, water damage, writing, or tears. Still sells, but as a "player" copy — a fraction of Near Mint prices.

Two things people miss

Centering. If the borders are noticeably wider on one side, that alone can cap a grade — even on a card with no wear at all.

Sealed isn't automatically mint. Crushed corners, dented boxes, torn or resealed shrink wrap, and sun-faded packaging all matter. Judge the box the same way you'd judge a card.
