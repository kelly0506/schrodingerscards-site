    What’s out there

    Rarer cats are faster, twitchier, and need a longer hold, with a tighter ring to stay inside. They also turn up far less often — though the odds shift toward them as the round goes on, so the back half is where the big scores are. Uncaught cats wander off after a while, so a gold sighting is worth dropping whatever else you were doing.
