Our Story

Two collectors, rediscovering the hobby they grew up with.

Schrödinger's Cards was founded by a husband-and-wife team who both collected Pokémon cards as kids. In early 2026, rediscovering our own childhood collections reignited that passion — and cataloging them properly took months of careful work: identifying sets, verifying condition, and researching real market values. That process gave us a firsthand, detail-level understanding of the hobby most shops don't have.

What started as reselling and grading our own cards has grown into a full retail operation in a matter of months. We've since formalized as Schrödinger's Cards LLC to support the next stage of growth. Between us, we also bring over a decade of professional experience in online retail, marketing, and data analytics — which we put directly to work in how we source, price, and stock inventory.

Visit Us

A card shop in an antiques & collectibles mall.

Rather than a standalone card shop, you'll find us at Booth 151 inside Treasure Cove Mall in downtown Jacksonville, Texas — a browsing-friendly antiques and collectibles mall with dozens of vendors. We built the booth to be family-friendly and community-oriented: a place where kids discover cards as collectibles, not just as a game, and parents can browse right alongside them.

If you've never sought out a dedicated card shop before, that's exactly who we're for — come see what you find.
