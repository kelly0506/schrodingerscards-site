High scores

    Scoring. Damage is how much of the level ended up broken, moved or awake. Points are damage times a multiplier that rises with the level, so a good poke late is worth several early ones. The last level is a city and the cats are the size of buildings. The towers have health: hit one hard enough and its lights go out and it cracks, and enough of that brings it down on whatever was standing on it. A good cat takes down four of the six; only the glowing one takes all of them. One cat is glowing — poke that one directly and it does something the others cannot. The chain reaching it will not do; you have to choose it.
