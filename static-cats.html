Static Cats

Press and scrub side to side — finger or mouse, it is the same gesture — and the cat charges up until his fur is standing on end. Charged fur picks up balloons. Below are three different games built on that one idea: the cat, the scrubbing and the balloons are identical in all three, and everything else is deliberately not. Play a room of each and see which one you want to keep.

Static Cats

Press and scrub side to side to charge him up.

What is the same in all three. The cats are the eight from the other games, and the spiky fur is the same code that makes a Catastrophe cat puff up when it panics — here it is wired to the charge meter instead, so the cat is the readout. Charge always leaks away, so a charged cat is a temporary cat. Roughly one balloon in five has something rattling about inside it: five more seconds, a full charge, a slow-drift spell, or magnet paws. Balloons you have collected ride above you on strings and pull you upward, so a full cat steers like a balloon itself. What differs is where charge comes from and what it does — A spends it, B makes it a place you have to go back to, C turns it into a magnet with a window rather than a key. Nine rooms, one clock across all of them, twelve seconds handed back for clearing a room.
