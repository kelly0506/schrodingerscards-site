A small diversion

Hat in the Cat

Cats contain multitudes, and one of them is a hat. Your cursor is a small x-ray lens — sweep it over the cat to see what is in there, and when you find the hat, press and hold to pull it out. Hold on the wrong thing and it costs you time, so be sure before you commit. Sixty seconds, as many cats as you can get through.

Find the hat

Move the lens over the cat to see inside. Press and hold on the hat to pull it out and move on to the next cat. Holding on anything else costs you four seconds — and there are things in there worth finding on purpose.

High scores

    How it gets harder. Each cat holds more than the last — ten items to start, up to forty — the hat shrinks, and the other things spread out in size until the inside is a proper jumble. From the third cat there are also shapes that read as a hat for half a second and are not. Pick-ups shine, so you always know one when you pass it. The lens ring warms up when the hat is close — a confirmation rather than a compass, since it only reads within about a lens and a half. Every cat is a different animal: six builds, six coats and five resting faces, which is a hundred and eighty combinations before you count the ears.
