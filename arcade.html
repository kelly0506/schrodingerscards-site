You found it

Three small games

Nothing to do with cards, and no reason for any of them to exist. Each keeps its own high scores. Pick one.

Reflexes · 45 seconds

Schrödinger’s Cats

Your cursor is a wave function. Hold it on a cat until it collapses into your collection. The gold ones are fast, twitchy and worth fifty commons.

Search · 60 seconds

Hat in the Cat

Every cat has a hat somewhere inside it. Your cursor is an x-ray lens — sweep, find it, and hold to pull it out. Hold the wrong thing and it costs you.

Judgement · nine lives

If I Fits I Sits

A cat is a liquid. Pick the vessel it fills most exactly and watch it pour itself in — it never changes size, so a narrow vessel just makes it taller.

None of them are linked from anywhere else on the site. The way back in is the same way you got here: the mark in the top left.
